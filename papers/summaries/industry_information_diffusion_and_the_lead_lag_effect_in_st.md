# Industry Information Diffusion and the Lead-Lag Effect in Stock Returns

**Authors:** Kewei Hou  
**Year:** 2007  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=463005

> Big firms' returns lead small firms' returns within the same industry; slow diffusion of industry news explains most of the size-based lead-lag effect.

## Findings
- Intra-industry lead-lag dominates the cross-industry effect. — *RFS 20(4), US stocks 1963-2001.*

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Slow information diffusion to small, less-followed firms.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1963-2001  Frequency: weekly  Holding: 1 week

## Reported results
Big firms' returns lead small firms' returns within the same industry; slow diffusion of industry news explains most of the size-based lead-lag effect.
