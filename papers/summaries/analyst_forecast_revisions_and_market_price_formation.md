# Analyst Forecast Revisions and Market Price Formation

**Authors:** Cristi A. Gleason, Charles M.C. Lee  
**Year:** 2003  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** https://iro.uiowa.edu/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/Analyst-Forecast-Revisions-and-Market-Price/9984380448602771

> Prices under-react to analyst forecast revisions that carry new information (vs those that just move toward consensus); adjustment is faster for celebrity analysts and widely covered firms, and much of the delayed move happens at later earnings announcements.

## Findings
- Market doesn't separate high-innovation from low-innovation revisions. — *The Accounting Review 78(1).*
- Faster adjustment for All-Star analysts and high-coverage firms.
- Delayed adjustment clusters around later announcements.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Slow information diffusion.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: n/a  Frequency: monthly  Holding: months

## Reported results
Prices under-react to analyst forecast revisions that carry new information (vs those that just move toward consensus); adjustment is faster for celebrity analysts and widely covered firms, and much of the delayed move happens at later earnings announcements.
