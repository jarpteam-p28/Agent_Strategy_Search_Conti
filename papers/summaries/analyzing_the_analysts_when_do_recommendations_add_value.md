> Analysts favour glamour stocks (momentum, growth, high volume, high valuations); recommendation levels only predict returns for stocks with good quant traits (value, positive momentum), and the quarterly CHANGE in consensus is the more robust predictor.

## Findings
- Consensus level predicts returns only for value / positive-momentum stocks; for the rest, higher ratings → weaker returns. — *Journal of Finance 2004.*
- Quarterly change in consensus is a robust, incremental predictor.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Analyst incentives / glamour bias.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: n/a  Frequency: quarterly  Holding: months

## Reported results
Analysts favour glamour stocks (momentum, growth, high volume, high valuations); recommendation levels only predict returns for stocks with good quant traits (value, positive momentum), and the quarterly CHANGE in consensus is the more robust predictor.
