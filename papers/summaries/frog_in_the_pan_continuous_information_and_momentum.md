# Frog in the Pan: Continuous Information and Momentum

**Authors:** Zhi Da, Umit G. Gurun, Mitch Warachka  
**Year:** 2014  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1745247

> Momentum is much stronger in stocks whose past return came from many small moves ('continuous information') than from a few big jumps; investors under-react to information that arrives gradually.

## Findings
- Continuous-information winners/losers drive momentum; discrete-information momentum is weak. — *RFS 27(7), US stocks 1927-2007.*

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Limited attention to small, frequent changes.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1927-2007  Frequency: monthly  Holding: 6-12 months

## Reported results
Momentum is much stronger in stocks whose past return came from many small moves ('continuous information') than from a few big jumps; investors under-react to information that arrives gradually.
