# Demand Curves for Stocks Do Slope Down

**Authors:** Andrei Shleifer  
**Year:** 1986  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** None

> Stocks added to the S&P 500 (1976-1983) jumped ~3% on the announcement and the gain did not fully reverse, evidence that demand curves for stocks slope down (index-fund buying moves prices).

## Findings
- Announcement-day abnormal return ≈ +2.8% for additions after 1976 as index funds grew.
- No full reversal over the following weeks.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Price pressure from mandated index buying.
