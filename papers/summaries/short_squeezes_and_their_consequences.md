# Short Squeezes and Their Consequences

**Authors:** Paul Schultz  
**Year:** 2024  **Type:** academic  **Read:** secondary  
**Source:** https://www.evidenceinvestor.com/post/the-consequences-of-short-squeezes

> Short squeezes (lenders recalling shares) are rare except in high-utilization, high-fee stocks, where they cost short sellers 56-73 bp a month and eat more than two-thirds of the −1.29%/month return of those stocks.

## Findings
- At ≥ 90% utilization, a squeeze roughly every 11 days; at ≤ 25%, once in 40 years. — *IHS Markit lending data 2006-2019, JFQA.*
- Squeeze costs 0.87-1.04%/month vs −1.29%/month excess return for high-utilization stocks.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Limits to arbitrage via recall risk.
