# A Flow-Based Explanation for Return Predictability

**Authors:** Dong Lou  
**Year:** 2012  **Type:** academic  **Read:** full_pdf  
**Source:** https://www.fmg.ac.uk/sites/default/files/publications/DP643.pdf

> Mutual-fund flows create predictable price pressure in the stocks funds hold: flow-induced pressure earns ~5% the next year and reverses (−5.7%) in years 2-3; this explains fund performance persistence and the 'smart money' effect and part of stock momentum.

## Findings
- Managers cut holdings ~1:1 on outflows (0.97) but only partly add on inflows (0.62). — *RFS 25(12), US funds 1980-2006.*
- Expected flow-induced price pressure decile spread +5.28% next year, −5.67% quarters 6-12.
- Controlling for it cuts the momentum coefficient 25-50%.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Flow-driven demand shocks with later reversal.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1980-2006  Frequency: quarterly  Holding: 1-3 years

## Reported results
Mutual-fund flows create predictable price pressure in the stocks funds hold: flow-induced pressure earns ~5% the next year and reverses (−5.7%) in years 2-3; this explains fund performance persistence and the 'smart money' effect and part of stock momentum.
