# Premium for Heightened Uncertainty: Explaining Pre-Announcement Market Returns

**Authors:** Grace Xing Hu, Jun Pan, Jiang Wang, Haoxiang Zhu  
**Year:** 2022  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** https://www.nber.org/papers/w25817

> Large overnight market returns occur before nonfarm payrolls, ISM and GDP releases, similar to pre-FOMC returns and with no abnormal variance; they reflect the build-up and resolution of 'impact uncertainty' measured by VIX.

## Findings
- Large overnight returns before NFP, ISM and GDP announcements, similar to pre-FOMC. — *JFE 145(3), 2022.*
- No abnormal variance in those windows.
- Returns tied to VIX build-up before and drop at the announcement (impact uncertainty).

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Risk premium for heightened uncertainty about how much the news will move markets.

Uncertainty builds before scheduled news, investors demand a premium, it resolves just before the release.


## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: S&P 500 / E-mini
- Period: not stated in abstract  Frequency: intraday/overnight  Holding: overnight before release

## Reported results
Positive pre-announcement overnight returns before NFP/ISM/GDP.

## Tradable edge (our framing)
Long ES overnight (16:00 close → 08:30/10:00 ET release) before NFP, ISM and GDP release days; stronger when uncertainty is high.
