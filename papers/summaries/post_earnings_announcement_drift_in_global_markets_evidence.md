**Authors:** Mingyi Hung, Xi Li, Shiheng Wang  
**Year:** 2015  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only

## Findings
- PEAD declines after the information shock. — *RFS 28(4), 2015.*
- Decline larger with fewer concurrent announcements, more institutions, lower limits to arbitrage.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Limited attention / mispricing.




## Setting
- Markets: Global equities
- Instruments: non-US stocks
- Period: n/a  Frequency: quarterly  Holding: months

## Reported results
When reporting quality improved sharply for non-US firms (an exogenous information shock), PEAD declined — more so where attention was less split, institutions owned more and arbitrage was easier — supporting limited attention as the cause of PEAD.
