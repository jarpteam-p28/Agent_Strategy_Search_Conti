# Reversal, Momentum and Intraday Returns

**Authors:** Haoyu Xu  
**Year:** 2017  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://www.cxoadvisory.com/momentum-investing/when-to-look-for-momentum-and-reversal-intraday/

> In US stocks (1993-2014), momentum is a morning effect (first-2-hour momentum alpha 0.82%/month) and reversal an afternoon effect (last-2-hour reversal alpha 0.59%/month); both weakened in 2004-2014.

## Findings
- First 2 hours (9:30-11:30) momentum: 4-factor alpha 0.82%/month vs 0.36% conventional. — *Stocks > $5, top 90% NYSE caps.*
- Last 2 hours (14:00-16:00) reversal: alpha 0.59%/month; first-2-hour reversal negative.
- Last-2-hour reversal alpha fell from 0.96% (1993-2003) to 0.35% (2004-2014). — *Gross; 28 windows tested.*

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Information trading in the morning, liquidity trading in the afternoon.
