# Intraday Trading Invariance in the E-mini S&P 500 Futures Market

**Authors:** Torben G. Andersen, Oleg Bondarenko, Albert S. Kyle, Anna A. Obizhaeva  
**Year:** 2016  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/academics-research/research/detail/2016/intraday-trading-invariance-in-the-e-mini-sp-500/

> In E-mini S&P futures (2008-2011) return variation per transaction is log-linearly related to trade size with slope −2, across the intraday pattern and across days — a microstructure regularity, not a trading signal.

## Findings
- Return variation per transaction vs trade size: log-linear slope −2. — *ES, Jan 2008-Nov 2011.*
- Holds through the intraday pattern and across days.
