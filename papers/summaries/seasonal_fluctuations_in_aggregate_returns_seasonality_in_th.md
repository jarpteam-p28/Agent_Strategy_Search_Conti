# Seasonality in the Cross-Section of Stock Returns

**Authors:** Steven L. Heston, Ronnie Sadka  
**Year:** 2008  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://www.rhsmith.umd.edu/news/seasonality-stock-market-returns

> Stocks that did well in a given calendar month tend to do well in the same month in later years, for up to 20 years (decile spreads > 60 bps/month, NYSE/AMEX 1963-2002).

## Findings
- Same-calendar-month returns persist up to 20 years; decile spreads > 60 bps/month. — *NYSE/AMEX monthly 1963-2002 (JFE 2008).*
- Positive in all 12 months; strongest in January, above average in October and December. — *Not explained by size, industry or earnings timing.*
