# The Earnings Announcement Premium Around the Globe

**Authors:** Brad M. Barber, Emmanuel T. De George, Reuven Lehavy, Brett Trueman  
**Year:** 2013  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch

> Across 46 markets (1991-2010) a long-announcers / short-non-announcers portfolio earned 7.16%/yr with a 40% higher Sharpe than the market; the premium tracks the rise in idiosyncratic uncertainty around announcements.

## Findings
- 7.16%/yr raw; significant in 9 of the 20 biggest countries (63-236 bps). — *~200,000 announcements.*
- Driven by uncertainty about the disclosure, not attention or limits to arbitrage. — *~100% monthly turnover.*

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Uncertainty premium around scheduled information.
