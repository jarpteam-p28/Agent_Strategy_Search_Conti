# The Earnings Announcement Premium and Trading Volume

**Authors:** Andrea Frazzini, Owen A. Lamont  
**Year:** 2007  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://www.nber.org/digest/mar08/stocks-rise-around-earnings-announcements

> Stocks rise around their earnings announcements; buying expected announcers and shorting non-announcers earned > 60 bps/month (1972-2004), strongest where announcement volume (small-investor buying) is high.

## Findings
- Long expected announcers / short non-announcers: > 60 bps per month. — *CRSP 1972-2004; premium back to 1927.*
- Larger for stocks with big predicted volume surges and small-investor buying.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Attention-driven buying pressure around announcements.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1972-2004  Frequency: monthly  Holding: 1 month
