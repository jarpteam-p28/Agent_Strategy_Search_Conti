# Earnings Announcements are Full of Surprises

**Authors:** Michael W. Brandt, Runeet Kishore, Pedro Santa-Clara, Mohan Venkatachalam  
**Year:** 2008  **Type:** academic  **Read:** secondary  
**Source:** http://ssrn.com/abstract=909563

> The stock's abnormal return around the announcement (EARS) predicts drift better than the accounting surprise (SUE); EARS drift is larger, persists, and survived after the SUE drift shrank.

## Findings
- Return-based surprise drift larger and more persistent than SUE drift. — *US stocks 1987-2004 (Quantpedia summary).*

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Investor under-reaction to the information in the market's own reaction.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1987-2004  Frequency: daily  Holding: 60 days

## Reported results
