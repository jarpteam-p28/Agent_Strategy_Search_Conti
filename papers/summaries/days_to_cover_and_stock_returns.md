# Days to Cover and Stock Returns

**Authors:** Harrison Hong, Frank Weikai Li, Sophie X. Ni, Jose Scheinkman  
**Year:** 2015  **Type:** academic  **Read:** secondary  
**Source:** https://www.cxoadvisory.com/short-selling/days-to-cover-short-interest-as-a-stock-return-predictor

> Days-to-cover (short interest ÷ daily turnover) predicts lower stock returns far better than short interest alone: equal-weighted long-short 1.19%/month, Sharpe 1.3 (1988-2012), positive in 23 of 25 years, gross.

## Findings
- DTC long-short 1.19%/mo EW (0.67% VW) vs 0.71% for short ratio. — *NBER w21166.*
- DTC subsumes the short-interest ratio after 2000.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Shorting cost/crowding measured relative to liquidity.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1988-2012  Frequency: monthly  Holding: 1 month

## Reported results
Days-to-cover (short interest ÷ daily turnover) predicts lower stock returns far better than short interest alone: equal-weighted long-short 1.19%/month, Sharpe 1.3 (1988-2012), positive in 23 of 25 years, gross.
