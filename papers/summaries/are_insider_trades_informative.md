# Are Insider Trades Informative?

**Authors:** Josef Lakonishok, Inmoo Lee  
**Year:** 2001  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v14y2001i1p79-111.html

> Across NYSE/AMEX/Nasdaq 1975-1995, insider purchases (not sales) predict returns, mainly at small firms; insiders are contrarian and time the market better than simple contrarian rules, with little market reaction when trades are reported.

## Findings
- Informative signal comes from purchases; sales show no predictive ability. — *RFS 14(1), 2001.*
- Driven by small firms.
- Insiders as a group forecast market moves better than simple contrarian strategies.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Insider information.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1975-1995  Frequency: monthly  Holding: months

## Reported results
Across NYSE/AMEX/Nasdaq 1975-1995, insider purchases (not sales) predict returns, mainly at small firms; insiders are contrarian and time the market better than simple contrarian rules, with little market reaction when trades are reported.

## Tradable edge (our framing)
Stock-level; aggregate insider buying as a market-timing signal is untested here.
