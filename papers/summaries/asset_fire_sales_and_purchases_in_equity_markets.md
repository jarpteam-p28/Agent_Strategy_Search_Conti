# Asset Fire Sales (and Purchases) in Equity Markets

**Authors:** Joshua Coval, Erik Stafford  
**Year:** 2007  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://www.cxoadvisory.com/mutual-hedge-funds/going-with-the-flows/

> Extreme mutual-fund outflows force predictable selling that depresses prices for about two quarters before reversing; front-running forced sales and purchases earned > 15%/yr (1980-2003).

## Findings
- Flow-driven trades are predictable from fund performance and holdings; price effects last ~2 quarters, then unwind. — *1980-2003.*
- Front-running strategy > 15% annual excess (mostly from inflows).

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Institutional constraints: forced trading by funds facing flows.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1980-2003  Frequency: quarterly  Holding: quarters
