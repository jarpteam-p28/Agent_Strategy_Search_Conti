# The Russell Reconstitution Effect

**Authors:** Ananth Madhavan  
**Year:** 2003  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://www.cxoadvisory.com/miscellaneous/strategies-for-exploiting-index-rebalancing/

> Russell index reconstitutions (1996-2001) produced economically and statistically significant abnormal returns from temporary price pressure and permanent liquidity changes, with very high volatility on reconstitution day; later work finds the effect and its reversals weakening.

## Findings
- Significant abnormal returns around June reconstitution, 1996-2001. — *Financial Analysts Journal 59(4), 2003 (2001 working paper).*
- Additions gain on the reconstitution date rather than the announcement; deletions negative. — *Follow-up studies.*
- Effect and reversals much smaller in recent years. — *2007 follow-up.*
