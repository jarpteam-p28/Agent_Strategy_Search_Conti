# Can Investors Profit from the Prophets? Security Analyst Recommendations and Stock Returns

**Authors:** Brad M. Barber, Reuven Lehavy, Maureen McNichols, Brett Trueman  
**Year:** 2001  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v56y2001i2p531-563.html

> Buying the most-recommended stocks and shorting the least-recommended earned >4%/yr gross (1986-1996), but high turnover and costs wipe it out unless rebalanced infrequently.

## Findings
- Top-minus-bottom consensus portfolio > 4% annual abnormal return gross. — *JF 56(2).*
- Net of transaction costs the abnormal returns are not reliably positive.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Slow reaction to analyst information.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1986-1996  Frequency: daily  Holding: days-months

## Reported results
Buying the most-recommended stocks and shorting the least-recommended earned >4%/yr gross (1986-1996), but high turnover and costs wipe it out unless rebalanced infrequently.
