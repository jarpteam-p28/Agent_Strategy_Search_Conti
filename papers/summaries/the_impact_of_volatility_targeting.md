# The Impact of Volatility Targeting

**Authors:** Campbell R. Harvey, Edward Hoyle, Russell Korgaonkar, Sandy Rattray, Matthew Sargaison, Otto Van Hemert  
**Year:** 2018  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://www.cxoadvisory.com/equity-premium/benefits-of-volatility-targeting-across-asset-classes

> Across 60+ assets, volatility targeting raised Sharpe ratios mainly for equities and equity index futures (US stocks 0.40 → 0.48-0.51) and consistently reduced crash risk across nearly all assets.

## Findings
- US equities: Sharpe 0.40 → 0.48-0.51 with volatility scaling (10% target). — *Since 1927.*
- Sharpe improved only for equity index futures among futures; crash risk fell for nearly all. — *Futures since 1988.*
- Scaling truncates both tails.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Leverage effect in equities; volatility clustering lets you forecast risk.




## Setting
- Markets: multi-asset
- Instruments: 60+ assets
- Period: 1927-2017  Frequency: daily (5-min tested)  Holding: daily rebalance
