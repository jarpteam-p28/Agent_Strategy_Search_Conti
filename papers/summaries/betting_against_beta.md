# Betting Against Beta

**Year:** 2014  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only

> Leverage-constrained investors bid up high-beta assets, so a long low-beta / short high-beta (leveraged to beta-neutral) factor earns large risk-adjusted returns across US and international stocks, bonds and futures.

## Findings
- BAB factor Sharpe ≈ 0.78 in US stocks (1926-2012); positive in 18 of 19 countries.
- BAB returns fall when funding constraints tighten (TED spread).
