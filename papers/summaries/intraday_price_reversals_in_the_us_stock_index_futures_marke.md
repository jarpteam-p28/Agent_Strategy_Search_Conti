# Intraday Price Reversals in the US Stock Index Futures Market: A 15-Year Study

**Authors:** James L. Grant, Avner Wolf, Susana Yu  
**Year:** 2005  **Type:** academic  **Read:** abstract_only  
**Source:** https://digitalcommons.montclair.edu/acctg-finance-facpubs/70

> In US stock index futures (Nov 1987-Sep 2002), large price changes at the open are followed by highly significant intraday reversals (stronger after large up-opens), but the significance drops sharply after a bid-ask transaction-cost proxy.

## Findings
- Highly significant intraday reversals after large opening moves; present by year and weekday. — *Journal of Banking & Finance 29(5).*
- Overreaction stronger after large upward opens.
- After transaction costs, significance falls sharply; profitability an open question.
