# Does Academic Research Destroy Stock Return Predictability?

**Authors:** R. David McLean, Jeffrey Pontiff  
**Year:** 2016  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://www.cxoadvisory.com/big-ideas/effects-of-market-adaptation

> Across 97 published return predictors, returns were ~26% lower out of sample before publication and ~58% lower after publication; about 32% of the decline is due to publication itself, peaking 3-4 years later.

## Findings
- In-sample 0.58%/month; out-of-sample pre-publication 0.40% (−26%); post-publication 0.26% (−58%). — *97 predictors, to 2013.*
- ~32% decline attributable to publication; largest 3-4 years after.
- Bigger decay for stronger in-sample results and price/volume-only signals.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Market adaptation: publication attracts arbitrage capital.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: 97 anomalies
- Period: 1926-2013  Frequency: monthly  Holding: n/a
