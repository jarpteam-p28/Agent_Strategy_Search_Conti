# A Tug of War: Overnight versus Intraday Expected Returns

**Authors:** Dong Lou, Christopher Polk, Spyros Skouras  
**Year:** 2019  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://alphaarchitect.com/2015/02/overnight-momentum-vs-intraday-momentum/

> Momentum profits are earned almost entirely overnight (3-factor alpha 0.95%/month overnight vs 0.11% intraday), while size, value and most other premia are earned intraday — consistent with individuals trading at the open and institutions during the day.

## Findings
- Momentum: overnight alpha 0.95%/month (significant) vs intraday 0.11%. — *JFE 134(1), 2019.*
- Other anomalies mostly intraday; clientele 'tug of war'.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Investor clienteles: retail at the open, institutions near the close.




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: long sample  Frequency: daily open/close  Holding: overnight vs intraday

## Reported results
Momentum profits are earned almost entirely overnight (3-factor alpha 0.95%/month overnight vs 0.11% intraday), while size, value and most other premia are earned intraday — consistent with individuals trading at the open and institutions during the day.
