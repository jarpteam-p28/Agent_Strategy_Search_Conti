# The 52-Week High and Momentum Investing

**Authors:** Thomas J. George, Chuan-Yang Hwang  
**Year:** 2004  **Type:** academic  **Read:** web_fetch  
**Source:** https://alphaarchitect.com/52-week-high-q-factor-investment-model/

> Nearness to the 52-week high predicts stock returns better than past returns and does not reverse long-term; later work links it to profitability/investment (q-factor), and practitioners find it sensitive to holding-period choices.

## Findings
- Price / 52-week high dominates past returns in forecasting. — *Journal of Finance 2004 (1963-2001).*
- Later: q-factor model largely captures it in value-weighted samples. — *George, Hwang & Li.*

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Anchoring on the 52-week high (slow reaction near highs).




## Setting
- Markets: US equities
- Instruments: stocks
- Period: 1963-2001  Frequency: monthly  Holding: 6-12 months

## Reported results
Nearness to the 52-week high predicts stock returns better than past returns and does not reverse long-term; later work links it to profitability/investment (q-factor), and practitioners find it sensitive to holding-period choices.
