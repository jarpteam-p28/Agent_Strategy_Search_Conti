# Mind the Gap: The Non-Fundamental Role of Earnings Days

**Authors:** Azi Ben-Rephael, Steffen Hitzemann, Yuanyuan Xiao  
**Year:** 2024  **Type:** academic  **Read:** full_pdf  
**Source:** https://haslam.utk.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Ben-Rephael-Paper.pdf

> The part of an earnings-day return not explained by the earnings surprise (Return-Earnings Gap) is largely non-fundamental: it reverses — about 21% within a year and ~54% within three years — and distorts later analyst forecasts and anomaly returns.

## Findings
- REG long-short earns 10.4% on the announcement day (DGTW-adjusted). — *228,266 announcements, 1985-2018.*
- Reversal 9.9% of the day-t move within 21 days, 21.4% at 1 year, 54.5% at 3 years.

## The WHY
**Pattern:** Earnings days amplify non-fundamental moves as well as correcting mispricing.
