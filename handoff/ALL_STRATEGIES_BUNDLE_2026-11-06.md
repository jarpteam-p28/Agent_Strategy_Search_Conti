### 4. `noise_area_last_half_hour_es` — Noise-Area Close Continuation: last 30 minutes only (ES/NQ, long/short, time exit at 16:00 ET)
- **Spec:** `strategies/candidates/noise_area_last_half_hour_es.yaml` · verdict: backtest · data: yes
- **Finding:** On SPY 1-minute data from 2007 to 2024, a move away from the open that is larger than the 14-day average absolute move at the same clock time tends to keep going into the close. The paper's FAQ chart shows the trend P&L builds mostly from 10:00 to noon and from 14:00 to the close. Earlier work (Gao et al. 2018; Baltussen et al. 2021) finds the last half hour is predictable from the day's earlier move. Our angle, which neither paper tests: use only the noise-band break and enter late. If price is still outside the band at 15:30 ET, trade the last 30 minutes in the direction of the break.
- **Why:** Execution mechanics of forced players. Several flows have to trade near the close in the direction of the day's move. Options dealers who are short gamma re-hedge their delta, and the hedge size grows with the day's return. Leveraged and inverse ETFs rebalance into the close. Institutional VWAP/close-benchmark algos finish their parent orders, and MOC imbalances follow the day's direction. These flows are mandatory and scale with the size of the move, so a day that has left its normal time-of-day range by 15:30 is the day when that forced flow is largest. The noise band gives a volatility-normalised test of 'big enough move'. This sleeve isolates the part of the existing full-day noise-area trade that is most directly explained by forced flow. It has far fewer bars at risk and gives a test of the mechanism itself: if the last 30 minutes carry no edge, the gamma/rebalancing WHY is weak on ES.
- **Market:** instruments: ES; NQ; bar_size: 1min; session: RTH 09:30-16:00 America/New_York (data stored in UTC; convert with DST); universe: ES continuous front month 2013-12 to 2026-09; NQ 2021-07 to 2026-09 as a second market · direction: long_short
- **Entry long:** At the 15:30 ET decision bar (the close of the 1-min bar ending 15:30): ES close > UpperBand(15:30) AND close > session VWAP (from 09:30) -> buy 1 contract at the next bar open.
- **Entry short:** At the 15:30 ET decision bar: close < LowerBand(15:30) AND close < session VWAP -> sell 1 contract at the next bar open.
- **Filters:** Noise band: sigma(t) = mean over the last 14 RTH sessions of |close at minute t / RTH open (09:30) - 1|. UpperBand(t) = max(today's open, prior RTH close) * (1 + VM*sigma(t)); LowerBand(t) = min(today's open, prior RTH close) * (1 - VM*sigma(t)). Prior RTH close = close of the 15:59 ET bar the day before (16:00 equity close, not the 16:15/17:00 futures close).; One trade per day; no trade if the decision bar is missing.; Skip half-day sessions (early 13:00 close) and the first 14 sessions after the data start (warm-up for sigma).; Roll days: compute bands on the continuous back-adjusted series; skip the roll date if it is not handled.
- **Exit:** stop: Main version: none (a pure 30-minute master signal, so the exit cannot flatter the entry). Variant: exit on a 1-min close back through max(UpperBand, VWAP) for longs / min(LowerBand, VWAP) for shorts. · target: — · time: Flatten at the close of the 15:59 ET bar (the 16:00 ET equity close). · other: Always compare against the baseline of always going long ES from 15:30 to 16:00 every day, and against the same window traded in the direction of the day's open-to-15:30 return with no band (the Gao-style rule).
- **Parameters:** lookback_days=14 (test 10, 14, 30, 60, 90); vol_multiplier_VM=1.0 (test 0.8, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5); decision_time_ET=15:30 (test 15:00, 15:30, 15:45); vwap_confirmation=True (test true/false); stop_mode=none (test none, band+VWAP trailing); gap_reference_close=15:59 ET bar close (test 16:00 ET vs 16:14 ET futures settle-ish bar)
- **Variants (pre-registered):** Decision at 15:00 with a 60-minute hold; Long-only; Add a regime gate: prior-day RSI(5) < 50 or 14-day realised vol above its 1-year median (short-gamma proxy); Band-distance scaling: size by (price - band)/sigma
- **Extra checks:** Chronological split: in-sample 2013-12 to about 2023-07, out-of-sample to 2026-09; tune only in-sample.; Must beat the always-long 15:30-16:00 baseline and the no-band Gao rule on the same bars.; Report by year, with special attention to quiet years (2017, 2019, 2024) and to 2018, 2020 and 2022.; Report long and short legs separately.; Run NQ 2021-2026 with frozen ES parameters as a second-market check.; Daily P&L correlation vs noise_area_momentum_es, overnight_drift_long and drift_vwap_pullback.; Outlier dependence: what share of profit comes from the top 20 days?; Pre/post 2022 split for the 0DTE regime.
- **Data needs:** ES 1-min RTH bars 2013-12 to 2026-09 (clean_ES_master.parquet); NQ 1-min 2021-07 to 2026-09; US exchange holiday and half-day calendar; Optional: VIX daily for a regime check (not in inventory)
- **Notes:** The full-day noise-area strategy is already specced as noise_area_momentum_es. This spec is a deliberately narrower angle that tests the into-the-close mechanism, not a copy.; Fixed 30-minute holding window, following the master-signal discipline from transcript 04.

### 9. `drift_vwap_pullback` — Drift VWAP Pullback (NQ, prop-firm challenge strategy)
- **Spec:** `strategies/conti/06_drift_vwap_pullback.yaml` · verdict: Conti · data: partial

When the session VWAP shows an established drift (price on one side of VWAP, VWAP sloping that way, and price moved at least 0.1% in the last hour), the first pullback candle is a high-probability entry in the drift direction. Reported: 300%+ over 4,000+ trades, 64% win rate, developed on 2020-2024 data and out-of-sample from 2024 to Aug 2026.
- **Why:** Execution traders at banks and brokers fill 90-95% of portfolio managers' orders with VWAP algorithms. Those algos trade most heavily when price is near VWAP, because a fill there is most likely to match their benchmark. So near VWAP the imbalance between buying and selling algos is revealed, producing sharp moves in the direction of the side in control. The three conditions give the level (price vs VWAP), the direction (VWAP slope) and the speed (1-hour move).
- **Market:** instruments: NQ; bar_size: 5 min execution bars; trend checked on 15 min bars; session: RTH 09:30-16:00 America/New_York; VWAP anchored at 09:30, computed on the 15-min series and read on the 5-min chart · direction: long_short

### 10. `overnight_bias_orb` — Overnight Bias Opening Range Breakout (NQ 15-min, long/short)
- **Spec:** `strategies/conti/09_overnight_bias_orb.yaml` · verdict: Conti · data: partial
- **Finding:** Where the RTH open sits inside the overnight range sets the day's bias: open in the top third -> only longs; bottom third -> only shorts; middle third -> no trade. Then trade a breakout of the first 15-minute range in the bias direction when ADX shows a trend. High reward/risk (3:1), low frequency.
- **Why:** Overnight price discovery by participants who can trade before the cash open reveals direction. ### 23. `same_slot_seasonality_es` — Same-Slot Intraday Seasonality (ES/NQ 30-min slots, long/short, slot time exit)
